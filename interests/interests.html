Recently I've Been Up To!

Hi there! If you're on this page, it is really just about random activities I've done recently. Usually farmers markets, or day trips. I'll just be sharing little tid bits of my life here!

Cascade Day Trip

September 25, 2026

Starting with a fun fact: September 25 is now considered National Dolly Day!

Today my dad and I took the day off work to visit my grandma. On the way, we stopped so I could grab a dirty chai tea latte from one of my favorite coffee shops, Bigfoot Brew Coffee. They’re always amazing no matter what I order, and the customer service is consistently quick and friendly.

We spent some time at the house before heading to Lakefront Bar & Grill at the golf course for an early dinner. I started with clam chowder, then had fish and shrimp with mac and cheese and tots on the side. Everything was delicious.

On the drive home, we ended up seeing rainbows everywhere. We spotted a double rainbow and could even make out both ends, and later we saw a full rainbow that we managed to photograph just before it faded. It was such a pretty way to end the day.

Nampa Farmers Market

August 29, 2026

We spent the morning walking around the Nampa Farmers Market and checking out all of the different booths. I usually already have a few regular vendors I like to visit, along with a couple new ones I want to try.

Today I tried a huckleberry roll from Bella's Bakery. It was similar to a cinnamon roll, but made with Idaho's staple berry, and it was absolutely delicious! I also got a freshly made strawberry hibiscus juice from Tere's Treats with a Tajín and chamoy rim.

We wandered around for a while and I somehow managed to avoid my usual wine tasting stop at Vizcaya Winery. They almost always have a booth with great wines to sample, but honestly, that's probably for the best considering I really do not need any more bottles in my collection right now.

Koe Wetzel Concert

July 23, 2026

After trying to see Koe Wetzel several times and somehow never having it work out, I finally made it to one of his shows! He’s easily one of my favorite artists from the current generation of country and rock, so I was ridiculously excited.

Bayker Blankenship opened the show, followed by Ole 60, and then finally Koe. I ended up running into quite a few people while we were there, which made the whole night even more fun.

At some point my feet were absolutely killing me, so I grabbed some amazing BBQ pulled pork mac and cheese and somehow found a chair tucked into a tree to sit and eat. Honestly, hiding in a tree with mac and cheese at a concert is probably one of the most accurate representations of me possible.

The whole show was amazing, and finally getting to see Koe live was absolutely worth the wait. I ended up getting sick afterward, which was unfortunate, but I’d still say the night was beyond worth it.
